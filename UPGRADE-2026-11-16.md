# Church Street Skate — upgrade 2026-11-16

Branch `upgrade-2026-11-16`. Implements the ranked plan in `ADVISOR.md` (items 1–8, all done)
against Stephen's brief: "it's too hard and repetitive… I want people to keep coming back."
Not pushed, not deployed. `main` is still what is live.

## What changed and why

### 1. The frozen skater (fixed)
Holding OLLIE off a kicker lip left `charging` stuck true for the rest of the run: no pushing,
no banking, charge bar lit. A release in the air (or rolling off a lip) now always ends the
charge, plus a belt-and-braces cancel when OLLIE is no longer held. A release within 0.15 s
of leaving the lip still pops (coyote time), so a late thumb isn't punished.
New check `feature-checks freeze` — fails on the old code (4.2 m/s, bar stuck), passes now
(18.1 m/s, bar off).

### 2. Fair, not punishing
- Landing: clean up to 0.80 rad (was 0.55), **sketchy** (combo kept, half speed, no spin
  points) up to 1.15 rad, slam only beyond. Assist 6 → 10 rad/s from 2.0 m (was 1.4 m).
  Analog spin needs > 60% stick (a thumb in a turn no longer spins you). A flip caught at
  60–82% lands sketchy instead of slamming.
- Bail 1.6 → 0.9 s; a bail keeps 60% flow (was 35%) and half the special meter (was 0).
- **Soft props**: bollards, granite posts, trash cans, sandwich boards, bike racks, bikes,
  hydrants, pay stations, bowl planters clack (−15% speed, a sound) instead of stopping or
  slamming you. Trees, lamp posts, walls and cars stay solid.
- Slam speed into walls 6.5 → 9 m/s; into low skate furniture (benches, planters, ledges,
  funbox sides) 13 m/s — below that you bounce. Pedestrians see you coming earlier (dodge
  radius 1.6 → 2.1 m, sidestep 1.0 → 1.35 m).
- Standing still holds you still (foot down) instead of creeping backwards down the grade.

### 3. Clear line, readable skater
- The 8 mall quarter pipes (walls across the street) are gone; manual pads moved into the
  rail lanes. The centre line is brick, kickers and funboxes only.
- Portrait camera: further back (5.0 m), higher (2.8 m), pitched down: the skater rides with
  his feet at ~55–61% of the screen, above the thumbs, and the street ahead fills the view.
- **See-through** (`js/seethrough.js`): café umbrellas, tree canopies, awnings, bunting,
  banners dissolve in a dithered hole around the skater when they are in front of him;
  decoration within 2 m of the lens isn't drawn. Shader approach, not the fallback.

### 4. Today's Line (the reason to come back)
Replaces the fixed 2-minute run. Same leaderboard id (`church-street-skate`), same single
`submitScore` at the buzzer, `js/leaderboard.js` untouched.
- **Five lines, one per local day** (`?day=0..4` pins one): Mall Bomb, College Hill, City
  Hall, The Waterfront, Main Street — each its own start and its own five B-T-O-W-N letters.
- **Letters are the breadcrumbs**: reset every run, a tall soft light column stands on the
  next one (visible from far down the street), each +500 and **+5 s**; all five +2,500.
- **Clock**: 90 s base (was 120), and it only starts when you first move. 5 letters = 1:55.
- **Three goals a day** (one easy, one medium, one hard, from a pool of 12, seeded by the
  date, line-aware): chips under the clock turn green; +1,500 each, +5,000 and LINE CLEARED
  for all three.
- **Last Call** at 0:20: clock goes red, every bank ×2.
- **Saves** (new keys only): `css-daily` {date, best, goals, runs}, `css-streak` {last, n},
  `css-run-best`. Old keys (`css-best`, `css-gaps`, `css-challenges`, `css-letters-*`,
  `css-ollied`, `css-flick`, `btown-player-*`) untouched — verified with a seeded profile.
- **Results**: score counts up, NEW BEST TODAY / NEW ALL-TIME BEST (only once there is a best
  to beat), **Again** right under the score (one tap, every orientation), the three goals
  with how-to hints, today's best · all-time · streak, and "Tomorrow: <line>".
- Free skate keeps the weekly letter route exactly as before.

What changes between… **minute 1 and minute 3**: letters extend the clock, goals pop, Last
Call doubles everything at the end. **Run 1 and run 10**: repeated tricks pay less (item 5),
goals and today's best to beat. **Today and tomorrow**: a different line, start and goals.

### 5. No paying for the same trick forever
Per-run staleness by trick name: first use ×1.5 (shown **FRESH** in green in the combo line),
then ×1, ×0.7, ×0.45, ×0.25, floor ×0.1 (worn-out tricks show dimmed). Free skate forgets
every 60 s. "Big Drop" needs a real ≥1.5 m drop below the takeoff; "Big Air" needs a ramp or
grind takeoff; tricks popped below 3 m/s pay 25%.

### 6. Two buttons, HUD diet (touch)
- **OLLIE** (hold, let go) and **TRICK**: tap = kickflip (stick picks Hardflip / Varial
  Kickflip; on the ground it pops for you), double-tap = 360 Flip (The Maple Leaf with the
  gold meter full), hold = grab, keep holding through a landing = manual. SHOVE, GRAB, FLIP
  and the flick-pad toggle are gone on touch; keyboard and gamepad unchanged.
- HUD in portrait play: score + gold bar · clock + letters · three goal chips · pause. Gone:
  trick tape, flow meter, wreck toast, speed (touch), minimap and MAP (touch; free skate has a
  Map toggle in the pause menu), best during a run. Location shows 1.5 s on change. One
  callout at a time (1.6 s queue).
- Pause: Resume / Restart / Sound on top; today's goals with hints; collection folded into one
  "Collection · spots · gaps · challenges" row; one-line key legend on desktop.

### 7. Title in one tap, teach by doing
Eyebrow, logo, "3D skateboarding score attack", one big **TODAY'S LINE — <line>** button
(loading state lives in it), today's best / streak or the line's blurb, a small Free skate
link, the OSM/USGS credit. ~23 words outside the buttons (was a 747 px wall). First run
coaches one prompt at a time with the control pulsing: push → tap TRICK → hold OLLIE and let
go (`css-ollied` is the done flag).

### 8. Juice
Bank popups scale with the bank (small / normal / big / gold-flash mega over 5,000) and show
×2 in Last Call; the bank chime climbs with combo length; 120 ms hit-pause on stomps and new
gaps; haptics (10 ms land, 25 ms stomp, [30,40,30] bail) guarded for iOS; results count up
with a tick; Bebas Neue for score, clock, title and callouts.

### Also fixed (found while testing)
- **Stacked frame loops**: every "Skate again" started another animation-frame chain, so each
  retry added a full update + render per frame (5 retries = 6 renders a frame). Now one loop.
- Test scripts raced the boot (`#btn-play` is enabled before main.js runs): they now wait for
  the dev hooks. Added `window.__T` (start / step / state / skaterBox / setLeft / endRun),
  `__hold` (analog input), `__freshen`.

## Items done / skipped / changed

All eight items are done. Deviations, each on purpose:
- **Camera size**: the advisor targeted 90–110 px; I left it at ~125 px median. Bigger read
  better on a phone in every screenshot, and the street ahead is still visible.
- **Casual-bot target** (≤ 3 bails, < 30 bumps) is not met: 8 bails, 13–68 bumps. Bumps are now
  harmless bounces off furniture; the remaining bails are head-on walls/trees at 20+ m/s,
  full-lock spins, and pedestrians. Making those free too would remove the last threat in the
  game, so I stopped here.
- **Line coordinates**: the advisor's sketches put several first letters on the start point,
  the City Hall letters on lawn and through the building, and the College Hill letters off the
  road (the old weekly route's last letter sits behind a row of buildings). All five lines
  were re-laid and driven start to last letter by an autopilot: Mall Bomb 32 s, College Hill
  25 s, City Hall 32 s, The Waterfront 44 s, Main Street 16 s, each leaving most of the clock
  for tricks.
- **Ticker**: this repo has no ticker script tag (the advisor said not to add one). The game
  calls `window.BtownTicker.hide()` on play and `.show()` on menus, guarded, so it works if
  the tag is ever added.
- The flick pad still drives the gamepad right stick; only its touch toggle is gone.

## Known issues
- **Score scale**: letters, goals and FRESH bonuses push a run to ~15–50k, vs ~10–15k for the
  old 2-minute run, so November's board mixes old and new scores. December starts clean.
- Mall draw calls on the phone tier peak at 111 (budget 110); the light column is +1.
- The local `python3 -m http.server` occasionally resets a module request, leaving the title on
  "Building Burlington…" (seen ~1 boot in 8 here; the harness retries). Not seen on a real
  host, but a failed module load still hangs with no message (pre-existing).
- On College Hill an overshot landing can drop you into a walled pocket south of College St
  near x −158; you can steer out, but the bot couldn't.
- Played in Chromium phone emulation with real CDP touches, not on a real iPhone/Safari.
- In landscape the results card scrolls to reach the leaderboard (Again is at the top).
